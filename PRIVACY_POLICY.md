# 调姬 (Debugirl) Chrome Extension Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2025-04-21

## Introduction

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for the "调姬" (Debugirl) Chrome Extension. This document outlines how our extension collects, uses, and protects information. We are committed to ensuring the privacy and security of all users.

## Information We Collect

### User-Provided Information
- **Account Information**: When you log in, we collect authentication information to allow you to use the extension's features.
- **Script Data**: Scripts created, modified, and executed through our extension are stored and synchronized with our servers when you enable those features.
- **Local Storage Data**: Data stored via the extension's local storage feature is saved on your device.

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Extension Usage Data**: We may collect basic usage statistics to improve our service.
- **Browser Tabs Information**: When you use features that interact with browser tabs, we access information about those tabs (URLs, content, etc.) to perform the requested functions.
- **Cookies and Web Storage**: Our extension can access, set, and modify cookies and web storage when running scripts you create or enable.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- To provide and maintain our service
- To allow you to create, store, and execute browser scripts
- To synchronize your scripts across devices when you're logged in
- To improve and optimize the extension's functionality
- To troubleshoot issues and provide support

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### Public Scripts
Scripts you mark as public will be available to other users in the script marketplace. Please do not include sensitive information in public scripts.

### Third-Party Services
We may use third-party services to:
- Host our backend infrastructure
- Process authentication
- Analyze usage patterns (in aggregated, non-personally identifiable form)

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by law or if we believe in good faith that such action is necessary to comply with legal processes.

## Your Data Control Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:
- **Access**: You can access your scripts and data through the extension interface.
- **Modification**: You can modify or delete your scripts at any time.
- **Local Storage**: Data in local storage remains on your device and is under your control.
- **Account Deletion**: You can request account deletion by contacting us.

## Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## Cookie Policy

Our extension may use cookies to:
- Maintain your session
- Remember your preferences
- Execute scripts that involve cookie manipulation (when you use such functionality)

Scripts you create may also set, modify, or delete cookies based on their functionality.

## Children's Privacy

Our extension is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

## Prominent Disclosure for Special Permissions

In accordance with Chrome Web Store policies, we prominently disclose that:

1. **Tab Access**: This extension can access your browser tabs for script execution purposes. It will only interact with tabs when you explicitly run scripts that use tab-related functions.

2. **Cookie Access**: This extension can access, modify, and set cookies when scripts you create use such functionality.

3. **Data Synchronization**: User scripts are synchronized with our servers when you log in and create or modify scripts.

4. **Remote Execution**: This extension allows remote script execution via API tokens that you create and control.
